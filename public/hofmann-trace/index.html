Hofmann Trace

A tool for drawing shapes on a grid of circles, in the manner of Armin Hofmann's exercises.

where the grid comes from

In his Graphic Design Manual (1965), the Basel designer Armin Hofmann presents a series of variations built on a grid of dots: shapes are born by joining circles with tangent lines, switching the direction of rotation each time. It is a system of remarkable economy — a circle, a straight line — that yields an endless family of silhouettes which all belong together. This tool takes that system and hands it to a rubber band: you circle a few dots, the band tightens and becomes an exact path of tangents and arcs.

resources

  • Armin Hofmann, Graphic Design Manual: Principles and Practice, Niggli, 1965 — variations 51 to 53.
  • Hofmann 1.0.0, bbtgnn's open-source tool where tangents are picked one by one, which was the starting point.
  • Developed with Claude and Codex.
